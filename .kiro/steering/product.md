# Product

A web platform for managing the urban housing rental cycle in Colombia (Valle del Cauca). The system acts as an orchestrator for the full rental process: property listing, tenant-landlord contact, contract formalization (with electronic signature), and payment management.

## Target Users

- Arrendador (landlord): typically older adults with low digital literacy who manage one or a few properties
- Arrendatario (tenant): typically younger adults with high digital adoption
- Anonymous user: can browse listings without authentication

## Core Value Proposition

Reduce friction in the rental process for users with low digital literacy, while remaining efficient for tech-savvy users. The main differentiator is digital inclusion — not just feature coverage.

## MVP Scope (Release 1)

- User registration and authentication (landlord / tenant roles)
- Property listing with mandatory photos
- Tenant search and filtering (zone/neighborhood)
- Contract upload and digital signing (via external e-signature provider)
- Rent payment via external payment gateway
- Payment history and confirmation
- Rental process status tracking
- Notifications for key events

## Out of Scope (MVP)

- Platform admin role
- Own payment processing engine
- Legal contract engine
- Debt collection / late payment management
- Risk scoring of tenants (planned for Release 3)

## Legal Context

- Ley 820 de 2003 (rental contracts)
- Ley 527 de 1999 / Decreto 2364 de 2012 (electronic signature)
- Ley 1581 de 2012 (personal data protection — Habeas Data)
